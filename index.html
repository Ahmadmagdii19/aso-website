01 · ABOUT

About ASO

ASO is one of the key pillars of the contracting sector in Saudi Arabia. For two decades we have delivered high-profile, strategically significant projects: high-rise towers, airports, universities, commercial centers, military facilities and luxury hotels. We do not merely construct buildings. We deliver fully integrated solutions.

A legacy of construction, a future of leadership

Through our Buildings and Construction Division we have delivered and managed a distinguished portfolio of projects to the latest construction systems and modern architectural standards. We are a classified contractor registered with the Contractors Classification Agency.

Our operations have expanded beyond the Kingdom into international projects, and from buildings into infrastructure, structural warehouses and residential units, with electrical, mechanical and landscaping works delivered by our own teams to the highest local and international standards.

Vision

To be a trusted leader in the construction industry, delivering world-class projects that contribute to sustainable development and support the Kingdom's Vision 2030.

Mission

  1. Deliver high-quality, cost-effective construction solutions
  2. Maintain the highest standards of safety, quality and sustainability
  3. Build long-term partnerships with clients and stakeholders
  4. Drive innovation through advanced construction technologies
  5. Develop and empower highly skilled teams

03 · HOW WE WORK

How we work

From tender to handover, one accountable team

Every project runs under one projects director, with our own engineers and crews on site.

1

Tender and pricing

We study your drawings, BOQ and scope, and return a priced proposal with a program and method statement.

2

Mobilization and permits

Work permits, material submittals and approvals are secured before we mobilize, including airside and high-risk permits.

3

Execution with our own crews

Civil, MEP, steel and fit-out works delivered by ASO teams and plant, under daily HSE supervision.

4

Quality control and handover

Inspection requests, testing and commissioning, as-built documentation and a clean handover.
